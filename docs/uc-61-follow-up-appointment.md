# UC-61, UC-62 — Follow-up appointment

**Status:** Proposed and implemented 15/11/2026 (the product owner accepted the proposals of [Decisions needed](#decisions-needed); they still need the team's decision record). Printing from a closed record (UC-52) and the lock at UC-58 are not implemented. Written from the project's business rules, schema and code and from TT 01/2025/TT-BYT; the draft UC-61 and UC-62 of Report 3 are **not** followed (product owner, 15/11/2026). Choices that differ from a business rule are listed under [Deviations from the sources](#deviations-from-the-sources) and need the team's decision record.

**Sources:** TT 01/2025/TT-BYT Điều 11, Điều 15 khoản 5, Phụ lục V ([`reference/TT-01-2025-TT-BYT.md`](reference/TT-01-2025-TT-BYT.md)); TT 26/2025/TT-BYT Phụ lục I chú thích 7 ([`reference/TT-26-2025-TT-BYT.md`](reference/TT-26-2025-TT-BYT.md)); CV 365/TTYQG-GPQLCL Phụ lục III.31.2 `loidanbacsi` ([`reference/CV-365-2025-TTYQG.md`](reference/CV-365-2025-TTYQG.md)); `Business_Rules_v7.xlsx` (BR-32, BR-34, BR-42, BR-47, BR-53, BR-108, BR-124); `usecases.xlsx` (UC-61, UC-62); `schema.yaml` table 31 `follow_up_appointments` (V7); [`uc-59-e-prescription.md`](uc-59-e-prescription.md) BR-59-08.

| Use case | Name | Scope here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UC-61 | Create Follow-up Appointment | The Doctor sets the follow-up date and advice of the visit, and changes them while the record is open. |
| UC-62 | Print Follow-up Appointment | The Doctor prints the Phiếu hẹn khám lại of TT 01/2025 Phụ lục V. |

Out of scope: the reception side of BR-34 (recognising the follow-up visit, "Đã đến khám", "Lỡ hẹn"), the electronic slip (digital signature, VNeID, XML14 of QĐ 4750/QĐ-BYT as amended by QĐ 3176/QĐ-BYT), and printing from a closed record (UC-52), which depends on UC-58.

## Description

When the patient needs further monitoring or a re-check of this visit's results "theo yêu cầu chuyên môn" (TT 01/2025 Điều 11), the Doctor of the visit records one follow-up appointment for the visit — the date and the advice — after saving the conclusion and before closing the record (UC-58). The Doctor prints the Phiếu hẹn khám lại and hands it to the patient with the clinic's stamp and the Doctor's signature. The appointment is recorded in the clinic's data so reception can match it later (Điều 11 khoản 3). A prescription sent after the appointment carries its date (BR-59-08, Điều 11 khoản 1 allows the date to be written in the prescription).

## Preconditions

- The Doctor is signed in (UC-01), has no pending password change, has role Doctor with status Active, is assigned to an active examination room and on duty there now, and the visit is the Doctor's own, in `IN_EXAMINATION` or `WAITING_CONCLUSION`, with an open medical record — the checks of `ConcludingVisitGuard`, as for UC-56 and UC-59.
- The conclusion of the visit is saved (UC-56): the slip carries its diagnoses (BR-61-05).

## Business rules

- **BR-61-01 (BR-34, TT 01/2025 Điều 11 khoản 5).** At most one appointment per visit record (`uq_follow_up_appointments_medical_record_id`). It is created by the first save and changed by later saves while it is `SCHEDULED` and the record is open. The Doctor decides whether a follow-up is needed; there is no check of a chronic-disease list (Điều 11: "theo yêu cầu chuyên môn").
- **BR-61-02 (BR-108).** Follow-up date: required; a valid date after the visit date (the date of `visits.thoigianvaovien` in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh) and at most 365 days after it. Advice (`loidanbacsi`, CV 365 III.31.2): optional, at most 2,000 characters, trimmed; empty is stored as null.
- **BR-61-03 (BR-53).** The slip number `so_giay_hen` is generated by the first save, is unique and never changes or is reused: `GH-` + the visit code `sovaovien`, e.g. `GH-DK0000000123` (**ASSUMPTION**: BR-53 names no format; the visit code comes from a counter and there is one appointment per visit, as the prescription's slip number `DT-` + visit code). `schema.yaml` says "sinh khi in"; it is generated at creation instead so the number exists before the first print and reception sees the same number.
- **BR-61-04 (BR-32, BR-47).** The appointment can be changed only while the record is open; UC-58 locks it with the record (owned by UC-58). A change after the prescription was accepted is valid but does not change the date on the sent prescription (BR-47, BR-59-08 option A); the form says so (MSG126).
- **BR-61-05 (TT 01/2025 Phụ lục V).** `maicd` keeps the conclusion's main diagnosis at each save. The printed slip reads "Chẩn đoán" (main diagnosis, code and name) and "Bệnh kèm theo" (secondary diagnoses) from the conclusion when it is printed; the conclusion cannot change after UC-58.
- **BR-61-06 (TT 01/2025 Điều 11 khoản 2, Phụ lục V).** The printed slip follows Phụ lục V:
  - header: "CƠ QUAN CHỦ QUẢN" (**TBD**: the facility settings of UC-77 have no such field; left blank), the facility name (`ten_cskcb`), "Số:" the slip number, the national motto, the title "PHIẾU HẸN KHÁM LẠI", and an empty area at the top left for the clinic's stamp ("dấu treo");
  - patient: name, sex ("Giới"), date of birth, address (address, ward, province);
  - insurance: "Số thẻ BHYT" and "Hạn sử dụng: Từ … Đến …" = the card number and the card's validity (`mabhyt`, `tungaybhyt`, `denngaybhyt`) of an insurance visit; blank for a self-paid visit;
  - "Ngày khám bệnh" = the visit date; "Ngày vào viện" and "Ngày ra viện" are for inpatients and are left blank (outpatient clinic);
  - "Chẩn đoán", "Bệnh kèm theo" (BR-61-05);
  - "Hẹn khám lại vào ngày … tháng … năm …, hoặc đến bất kỳ thời gian nào trước ngày được hẹn khám lại nếu có dấu hiệu (triệu chứng) bất thường." and "Phiếu hẹn khám lại chỉ có giá trị sử dụng 01 (một) lần. Trường hợp không đúng hẹn cần liên hệ với cơ sở khám bệnh chữa bệnh để được giải quyết." verbatim;
  - "Lời dặn" with the advice, when there is one (**ASSUMPTION**: not on Phụ lục V; added because the Doctor's advice belongs with the appointment, CV 365 III.31.2, TT 26 Phụ lục I chú thích 7);
  - "………, ngày … tháng … năm …" = the print date (the place is **TBD**: no field for it; left as dots), "Bác sĩ, Y sĩ khám bệnh", "(ký tên)", space for the signature, then the Doctor's academic title and name.

  The slip has **no validity period of the appointment**: Phụ lục V has none (see [Deviations](#deviations-from-the-sources) on BR-124). The paper slip needs the stamp and the handwritten signature to be valid (Điều 11 khoản 2); the system prints the space for both.
- **BR-61-07 (BR-42, CV 365 III.1.1.c–đ).** Audit rows in `audit_logs` on entity `follow_up_appointments`: `CREATE` with the date, advice and slip number; `UPDATE` with only the fields whose value changed, old and new, and no row when a save changes nothing; `VIEW_RECORD` when the Doctor opens an existing appointment; `PRINT` on each print, with `reprint` true after the first one; `printed_at` is set by the first print.
- **BR-61-08 (BR-47, BR-59-08).** The prescription copies the follow-up date of the `SCHEDULED` appointment when it is sent (implemented in UC-90). BR-47 says the appointment should be made before the prescription is sent; the tab shows MSG126 when the accepted prescription carries no date or another date.

## Normal flow — UC-61 Create Follow-up Appointment

1. The Doctor opens the "Hẹn tái khám" tab of the visit.
2. The system checks the preconditions and shows the visit date, the allowed date range (BR-61-02), the conclusion's diagnoses, the saved appointment if any (date, advice, slip number, status) and the prescription's sent follow-up date if the prescription was accepted.
3. The Doctor enters "Ngày tái khám" and, optionally, "Lời dặn tái khám", and clicks "Lưu".
4. The client checks BR-61-02 for feedback; the server checks the guard, the conclusion (EF-03), BR-61-02 and, for an existing appointment, the loaded `version`.
5. The server creates the appointment `SCHEDULED` with its slip number (BR-61-03), the visit, patient, Doctor and `maicd` (BR-61-05), or updates it, and writes the audit row (BR-61-07), in one transaction.
6. The system shows MSG04 ("Tạo thành công lịch hẹn tái khám.") on creation or MSG03 on a change, and the action "In giấy hẹn" (UC-62).

## Alternative flows — UC-61

- **AF-01 (step 2): The visit already has an appointment.** The form opens with it; step 3 changes it (BR-61-01). The slip number does not change. A slip printed before the change shows the old data: the Doctor prints again and takes the old slip back (**ASSUMPTION**: Điều 11 khoản 2 "mỗi phiếu chỉ sử dụng 01 lần" implies one valid slip).
- **AF-02 (step 3): Leave with unsaved changes.** "Hủy" or leaving the tab with unsaved changes asks MSG38; "Tiếp tục chỉnh sửa" returns to step 3, "Loại bỏ" restores the saved values.
- **AF-03 (step 2): The prescription was already accepted.** The form shows MSG126 with the date sent with the prescription (none or another date); saving is still allowed (BR-61-04).

## Normal flow — UC-62 Print Follow-up Appointment

1. The Doctor clicks "In giấy hẹn" on a saved `SCHEDULED` appointment.
2. The system builds the slip (BR-61-06) and shows the preview.
3. The Doctor clicks "In". The system writes the `PRINT` audit row, sets `printed_at` on the first print and opens the browser print dialog (printer integration TBD, HW-01).
4. The Doctor stamps and signs the paper slip (Điều 11 khoản 2) and hands it to the patient.

## Alternative flows — UC-62

- **AF-01 (step 2): Close the preview.** Nothing is printed or recorded.
- **AF-02 (step 1): No saved appointment, or unsaved changes.** "In giấy hẹn" is disabled with the hint "Lưu lịch hẹn trước khi in." (**ASSUMPTION** wording).

## Exception flows

- **EF-01 (any step): Guard.** No permission, wrong role or another Doctor's visit: `ACCESS_DENIED` (MSG13). No room or not on duty: `ROOM_ASSIGNMENT_REQUIRED`, `DOCTOR_OFF_DUTY` (MSG48). Visit not in examination: `VISIT_NOT_IN_EXAMINATION`. Record closed: `MEDICAL_RECORD_CLOSED` (MSG68).
- **EF-02 (UC-61 step 4): Invalid data.** `FOLLOW_UP_APPOINTMENT_INVALID` (422) with `field` and `reason`: date `REQUIRED` (MSG02), `NOT_AFTER_VISIT` (MSG33), `TOO_FAR` (MSG125); advice `TOO_LONG` (MSG08).
- **EF-03 (UC-61 step 4): No saved conclusion.** `FOLLOW_UP_CONCLUSION_REQUIRED` (409, MSG127).
- **EF-04 (UC-61 step 4): Changed meanwhile.** Wrong `version`: `CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION`; the Doctor reloads.
- **EF-05 (UC-61 step 4, UC-62 step 1): Not scheduled any more.** The appointment was cancelled by reception or used: `FOLLOW_UP_APPOINTMENT_NOT_SCHEDULED` (409, exists).
- **EF-06 (any step): Session expired or system error.** MSG12 or MSG80; nothing is saved.

## Postconditions

- **UC-61 success:** the visit has one `SCHEDULED` appointment with its slip number, date, advice and `maicd`; one `CREATE` or `UPDATE` audit row (none for an unchanged save). **Failure:** nothing changes.
- **UC-62:** one `PRINT` audit row; `printed_at` set on the first print; no other data changes.

## States

`follow_up_appointments.status` (V7): `SCHEDULED` (Đã hẹn) → `ARRIVED` (Đã đến khám, reception) / `MISSED` (Lỡ hẹn, end-of-day task) / `CANCELLED` (reception, with a reason). UC-61 creates and changes only `SCHEDULED`; UC-62 prints only `SCHEDULED`. The other transitions belong to reception and are not changed here.

## Schema changes

None. Table `follow_up_appointments` (V7) has every column used: `so_giay_hen`, `follow_up_date`, `loidanbacsi`, `maicd`, `status`, `printed_at`, `doctor_account_id`, `created_by_account_id`. The entity gets the domain methods `schedule`, `reschedule` and `markPrinted`.

## Deviations from the sources

| Source | Says | This document | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR-34, BR-124 | An appointment is used "từ ngày hẹn đến hết 10 ngày làm việc"; the slip carries the validity period | No validity period on the slip; the reception rules are not changed here | The 10 working days come from mẫu số 05 of NĐ 146/2018/NĐ-CP, whose forms TT 01/2025 replaced (Điều 15 khoản 5 điểm đ, at the latest 31/12/2025). Phụ lục V has no such period; "10 ngày làm việc" in TT 01/2025 is for the Phiếu chuyển (Điều 12 khoản 1). BR-124 stays an internal reception rule until the team revises it (Decisions needed 1). |
| BR-34 | A patient coming before the appointment date is registered as a new visit | Not changed here | Phụ lục V lets the patient come "bất kỳ thời gian nào trước ngày được hẹn khám lại nếu có dấu hiệu (triệu chứng) bất thường"; reception rule to revise (Decisions needed 1). |
| BR-34, `permission-matrix.md`, `schema.yaml` `maicd` | Only for periodic-monitoring patients (hypertension, diabetes) | The Doctor decides; no chronic-disease check | TT 01/2025 Điều 11: "theo yêu cầu chuyên môn" (Decisions needed 2). |
| `schema.yaml` `so_giay_hen` | Generated when printed | Generated by the first save | BR-61-03 |
| Report 3 draft UC-61, UC-62 | Validity period on the slip, MSG05 for a date beyond 365 days, no conclusion required | Not followed | Product owner 15/11/2026: specify from the project and the regulations |

## Decisions needed

1. **BR-34 and BR-124 against TT 01/2025.** Drop the 10-working-day validity (or keep it as an internal reception rule with the source corrected) and allow a visit before the appointment date to use the appointment when the patient has abnormal symptoms. Reception side; not implemented here.
2. **Who gets an appointment.** Record that the Doctor decides (TT 01/2025 Điều 11), and correct the Doctor/Follow-up row of `permission-matrix.md` and the `maicd` description in `schema.yaml`.
3. **Slip number format** `GH-` + visit code (BR-61-03).
4. **Conclusion required** before the appointment (EF-03), so the slip always has a diagnosis.
5. **"Cơ quan chủ quản" and the place** on the slip: add fields to the facility settings (UC-77) or leave them blank.
6. **Advice on the slip** although Phụ lục V has no line for it (BR-61-06).
7. **Message numbers** MSG125 → MSG128.
8. **Electronic slip** with the Doctor's digital signature (Điều 11 khoản 2, VNeID) and XML14 of QĐ 4750/QĐ-BYT: out of scope; could reuse the signing of UC-90 later.
