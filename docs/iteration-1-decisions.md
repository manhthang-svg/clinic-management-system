# Iteration 1 — Decision Register

## Document Authority

This document records the business and technical decisions that the Iteration 1 schema ([`iteration-1-schema.dbml`](iteration-1-schema.dbml)), Flyway migrations, and entities depend on. It started as the team's draft register of 36 questions (I1-D01 → I1-D36, reviewed 15/10/2026) and records how each one was resolved.

Source order follows [`business-context.md`](business-context.md#sources). Report 3 is cited as "R3" with its section or use case. Account-related technical decisions are recorded in this register or in [`security.md`](security.md); Report 3 remains authoritative for use-case behavior.

Status values:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Decided (R3) | Report 3 states the rule; adopted on 15/10/2026 |
| Decided (team) | The team chose between options on 15/10/2026 |
| Proposed | Technical proposal in the DBML, approved when the DBML review is accepted |
| Open | Not decided; the "Blocks" column says what waits for it |

## Scope

Iteration 1 covers UC-1 → UC-19, UC-35 → UC-38, UC-40 → UC-42 from the team's Phase 0 plan. The team additionally includes UC-74 so insurance verification can be completed by the registration flow (I1-D37 and I1-D38).

## Money Calculation

Decided by the team on 15/10/2026 (I1-D23, I1-D23a); revised on 08/11/2026 for the new BR-27 (I1-D43). Scope: every line of an examination-fee receipt and of a paraclinical receipt. Each line is one service, quantity 1. No drug charges (BR-33).

1. **Line amount.** Every line is charged at the clinic price, snapshotted when the line is created (BR-09).
2. **Cost within insurance scope.** The visit has a valid card and the service has an insurance price: the line is **insurance-covered** and its cost within scope is `min(insurance price, clinic price)`, snapshotted on the line. Every other case (self-paid visit, invalid card, service without an insurance price): 0, and the patient pays the clinic price in full.
3. **Applied coverage rate** of an insurance visit: 100% when the visit's total cost within scope (lines not cancelled, on receipts not cancelled) is below 15% of the base salary (`clinic.insurance-billing.base-salary`, default 2 340 000 VND, threshold 351 000 VND); otherwise the card's rate returned by the simulated portal (UC-74). No route (trái tuyến) or beneficiary-group recalculation.
4. **Insurance amount.** `round_half_up(cost within scope × applied rate)`, to whole VND. **Patient amount** = clinic price − insurance amount: the co-payment plus the difference between the clinic price and the insurance price. Only the insurance amount is rounded, so every total reconciles.
5. **Recalculation.** When a service is added to (UC-40, UC-49) or cancelled from (BR-25, not built yet) an open insurance visit, every unpaid receipt of the visit is repriced at the new applied rate; a receipt whose patient total becomes 0 is fee-exempt and returns to pending when the patient owes again. The rate is fixed when the doctor confirms the conclusion (UC-56, visit Completed). Each repricing that changes a patient amount is audited.
6. **Stored per line:** price type (`INSURANCE` or `CLINIC`), clinic price, cost within scope, applied coverage rate, insurance amount, patient amount.
7. **Receipt totals** are the sums of the lines. Exemption is decided per receipt, not per line: a receipt whose patient total is 0 is fee-exempt; a self-paid visit joins the waiting list immediately (BR-15) or its order is released immediately (BR-26).
8. **Database CHECKs** on each line: insurance amount ≥ 0; patient amount ≥ 0; insurance amount + patient amount = line amount; 0 ≤ cost within scope ≤ line amount; price type `CLINIC` implies insurance amount = 0 and cost within scope = 0.
9. **Simplifying assumptions** (stated in the report, like BR-21). The system does not apply: co-payment exemption after 5 consecutive years of coverage; service-specific payment rates; reduced rates for treatment outside the registered facility.

Report 3 must be corrected to match: UC-74 step 6 and §I.3.3 "Payment calculation" derive the coverage rate from the benefit code (100% / 95% / 80%); here the portal returns it.
